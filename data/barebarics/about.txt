Barebarics sneakers are a stylish choice for everyone who wants barefoot comfort in a modern, urban form. They are made for confident, bold people who are not afraid to stand out and show their style in full.

The collection features the iconic Zing, Wave, Revive, Legend, Urban Edge and Evo models for women and men. Each has its own character, yet they share the same barefoot DNA: an anatomically shaped wide toe box, a flexible sole, a zero drop and craftsmanship down to the detail.

They are stylish barefoot trainers that do not look like barefoot footwear at first glance. Pick your favourite and enjoy the urban comfort that goes with you every day.

What is Barebarics, and how is it related to Be Lenka?
Barebarics is a standalone brand by Be Lenka, created for people who were not won over visually by classic minimalist barefoot design. If you are after bolder city sneakers with the health benefits of a barefoot cut, Barebarics could be exactly your choice.

Barebarics offers the same principles as Be Lenka trainers. They have a wide toe box, a flexible sole and a zero drop. At the same time they bring a stronger sneaker silhouette, a more visible sole, bolder colour combinations and refined details, such as contrast laces, layered materials or interesting perforated elements.

That is why sneakerheads, lovers of urban style and anyone who wants to switch to barefoot without giving up a bolder design all find their pair in the collection.

What is the difference between Be Lenka and Barebarics trainers?
Be Lenka trainers are more minimalist, light and visually understated. They suit people who want a design as close as possible to the feeling of a bare foot.

Barebarics sneakers have a bolder urban look and resemble classic sneakers more closely. They often have a slightly chunkier but still flexible sole, stronger lines and bolder design elements. They remain true barefoot trainers, though, with an anatomically shaped toe box, a zero drop and a flexible build.

If you love stylish sneakers and want to switch to barefoot with no compromise on design, Barebarics is the natural choice.

Iconic Barebarics sneaker models
The collection has several iconic models worth getting to know first.

Zing is a versatile everyday model. With a classic sneaker silhouette, available in several colours, it is among the brand’s bestsellers.

Wave is a bolder, more colourful model for those who do not want to blend into the crowd. It tops off a city outfit beautifully and gives it character.

Revive is a year-round model in vegan materials with distinctive double lacing. Thanks to a breathable lining that keeps feet dry, it is ideal for active days in the city.

Legend draws on the relaxed world of skateboarding. A mix of leather and textile, a retro sole and an embroidered Urban Soul detail make it a striking year-round piece.

Urban Edge is a winter ankle model with a waterproof membrane and PrimaLoft insulation. It handles rain, snow and a full day of errands, keeping feet dry and warm.

Evo builds on leather and a bolder design that draws the eye. For those who want to make barefoot trainers the dominant element of an outfit.

Are Barebarics sneakers suitable for all-day wear and sport?
For city wear, yes. You can pull Barebarics trainers on in the morning and take them off in the evening. Thanks to the barefoot cut, the toes have plenty of room, the foot can work more naturally, and the zero drop helps keep a more natural posture.

For light sporting activity, walking, light jogging or a gym session, they can suit, depending on the model and your preferences. For sport, though, we recommend specialised models such as Velocity or Trailair, or take a look at our fitness footwear.

Barebarics is primarily a lifestyle brand. It shines most in the city, for everyday wear, work, walks and daily movement.

Customer reviews and experiences with Barebarics
Customers most often praise Barebarics sneakers for all-day comfort, a stylish look and quality craftsmanship. They frequently mention, too, that the trainers do not look like typical barefoot footwear, yet still offer all the benefits of a barefoot cut.

Favourite benefits include the wide toe box, flexible sole, zero drop, quality leather and long life. Beginners in barefoot footwear may need a short adjustment period, though. Feet gradually get used to the zero drop, the flexible sole and a more natural way of moving.

Many customers say that after the experience of Barebarics, they find it hard to go back to conventional trainers.
