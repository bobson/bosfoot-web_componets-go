Who are we?
Be Lenka is a European brand that changes people´s footwear habits worldwide thanks to its premium barefoot shoes. Since its establishment in 2017, it has won the hearts of hundreds of thousands of customers and has become a globally recognized leader in barefoot footwear. Led by the owner and CEO Juraj Fehervari, the brand achieved great international success and won several prestigious awards, including EY Entrepreneur of the Year. With more than 300 media references on the domestic and foreign scenes, Be Lenka is synonymous with innovation, quality and comfort. It´s not just a brand - it´s a lifestyle that provides freedom of movement without compromise.


What do we believe in?
We believe that barefoot shoes are not just a fashion trend, but a real health benefit and comfort for our customers. Using recent technologies and constant research and development, we create footwear that supports the foot´s natural function. Our shoes are designed to keep your feet fresh and rested throughout the day while helping to prevent foot fatigue and back pain. At Be Lenka, we believe that every step you take will bring you not only comfort but also a feeling of better health and well-being. We are certain that our shoes will help you feel good in your everyday life and bring you a unique walking experience.

The promise and the vision
Our customers can always count on Be Lenka to produce shoes that not only meet the highest standards but also bring joy with every step. We emphasize premium quality, carefully select the best materials and pay attention to perfect craftsmanship. We believe that comfort and health start with quality footwear, so we pay attention to every detail. Through the Be Lenka Foundation, we support various inspiring projects, and our mission is not only to produce beautiful and comfortable shoes but to contribute to a better future. With every step in Be Lenka shoes, you are taking a step towards a more responsible and sustainable world.



Research and development
Our vision is to constantly push the boundaries in the field of footwear using modern technologies and innovations. Our shoes are manufactured in leading European workshops, such as in Portugal, and by expanding production to Vietnam, we have gained access to global technological and production possibilities. This allows us to create footwear that highlights quality, comfort and sustainability. Our sole compounds are designed to provide unique properties, and we are striving to constantly push the quality of our products to a new level. We are determined to continue our mission to produce shoes with the lowest possible environmental impact and change the footwear habits of people worldwide. Today, our products are shipped to more than 80 countries, and we are continuously improving our processes, services, and the shoes themselves to bring you the best footwear in the world.
