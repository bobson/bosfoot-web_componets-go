What will you measure with?

  1. Place a sheet of A4 paper on a hard floor.
  2. Stand on it with your full weight, foot roughly in the middle. The whole sheet must stay visible.
  3. Take a photo from above — it doesn't need to be perfectly straight.
  1. Put any bank or ID card flat on a hard floor.
  2. Stand with full weight right next to it — card beside the middle of your foot, both fully visible.
  3. Take the photo from directly above — with the small card this matters more.
